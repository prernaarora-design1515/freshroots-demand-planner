# Next week's export (illustrative)

A second SAP export, one week after the seed (stock as of 26 Oct 2026, sales to 2026-W43), so the
app can show a second planning cycle and the 90-day results can move. It is **generated sample data**:

- W43 sales = each SKU's last-4-week average with a small (±4%) wobble.
- Dark Chocolate 80g (SKU014) jumps +45% in W43 (Diwali gifting), to show the demand-swing review.
- New launch: Kombucha 250ml (SKU016), 7 weeks of sales, to show the new-launch review.
- Stock = 19 Oct stock − W43 sales + arrivals of run #1's approved orders whose supplier lead time is
  7 days or less (Mango Drink 750, Almond Milk 150, Multigrain Bread 500). Brown Rice (10 days) and
  Peanut Butter (8 days) are still on order.

distributor_master.csv and calendar.csv are unchanged from the seed.
